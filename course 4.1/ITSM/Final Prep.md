# P5 - Risk Treatment and Mitigation Strategies

> **Risk Treatment** is the process of developing options and determining actions to enhance opportunities and reduce threats to the organization’s objectives.​

**Risk Treatment**
- Risk **Avoidance**
	- means eliminating the risk by not engaging in the activity that generates it.​
- Risk **Transfer**
	- stands for shifting the risk to a third party.​
- Risk **Mitigation**
	- means implementing measures to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.​
	- *Controls*
		- *Preventive* Controls
		- *Detective* Controls ​
		- *Corrective* Controls​
- Risk **Acceptance**
	- is defined as acknowledging the risk and choosing not to take any action to address it.​


> **Risk Treatment Plan** - a document that outlines how selected risk treatment options will be implemented.​

**Risk Treatment Plan**
- Prioritize risks
- select appropriate treatment options
- define treatment actions
- assign responsibilities
- allocate resources

# P8 - Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Procedure

- **BIA (Business Impact Analysis)**: Identifies critical business functions and evaluates the impact of disruptions to guide recovery and mitigation strategies.
- **BCP (Business Continuity Plan)**: A proactive plan to keep business running during disruptions.
- **DRP (Disaster Recovery Plan)**: A part of BCP focused on restoring IT systems after a crisis.

**BIA Steps**
- **Identify** critical business functions.
- **Assess** the impacts of disruptions.
- **Establish** recovery priorities.
- **Determine** 
	- Recovery Time Objectives (**RTO**).
	- Recovery Point Objectives (**RPO**).

**BIA Document**
- Initiating and Planning
- Data Collection
- Analysis of Information
- Identify Resource Requirements
- Report Findings

**BCP**
- Initiate the Project
- Conduct BIA
- Identify Preventive Controls
- Develop Recovery Strategies
- Develop the Plan
- Implement the Plan
- Test and Exercise the Plan
- Maintain and Review the Plan
